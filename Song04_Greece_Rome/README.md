# கிரேக்கம் & ரோமாபுரி | Greece & Rome – Rise and Fall of Mighty Empires | Album 37 Grand Finale Song 4

## Study Note
This song reflects on the glory and eventual decline of two of history’s greatest empires — Greece under Alexander the Great and the mighty Roman Empire. Even the most powerful human kingdoms that once ruled the world eventually crumbled. It powerfully points to Jesus Christ, who was crucified under Roman authority yet rose again, establishing an eternal Kingdom that will never fall.

## Prayer

English:
Lord Jesus, all earthly empires rise and fall, but Your Kingdom stands forever. Help us not to trust in worldly power, but in You — the King who conquered death and rules eternally. Amen. Hallelujah!

Tamil:

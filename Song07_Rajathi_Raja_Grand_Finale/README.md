# இயேசு கிறிஸ்து – இராஜாதி இராஜா | Jesus Christ – King of Kings and Lord of Lords | Album 37 Grand Finale Song 7

## Study Note
The climactic finale of the entire album and series. This song brings together the journey from Pharaoh to all empires and false gods, declaring the absolute, eternal supremacy of Jesus Christ. Every knee will bow and every tongue will confess that Jesus Christ is King of Kings and Lord of Lords forever.

## Prayer

English:
Lord Jesus, King of Kings and Lord of Lords, we worship You! All kingdoms, powers, and idols fall before You. Rule over our hearts, our lives, and this world. Come quickly, Lord Jesus. We crown You alone as our eternal King. Amen. Hallelujah!

Tamil:
